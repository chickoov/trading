# The Value Area Reclaim Scalp

## SECTION 1: Strategic Blueprint & Core Rules

### LONG Setup:
- **Entry Trigger**: Price closes above the VWAP + 2σ (Standard Deviation) band on a 5-minute chart, AND the FRVP POC is breached upward.
- **Confirmation**: Order Flow shows positive delta absorption at the VAH or High Volume Node. CVD divergence must be present (CVD > 0).
- **Exit**: Take profit at the next FRVP VAL or VWAP - 1σ; trail stop to VWAP on move.

### SHORT Setup:
- **Entry Trigger**: Price closes below the VWAP - 2σ band on a 5-minute chart, AND the FRVP POC is breached downward.
- **Confirmation**: Order Flow shows negative delta absorption at the VAL or Low Volume Node. CVD divergence must be present (CVD < 0).
- **Exit**: Take profit at the next FRVP VAH or VWAP + 1σ; trail stop to VWAP on move.

## SECTION 2: Fixed Range VP Tool Placement & Level Mapping

### Anchoring:
- Anchor FRVP to the last 3 RTH (Regular Trading Hours) session open, OR the most recent impulse wave high/low.
- **POC**: Midpoint of the anchored range; volume-weighted center of mass.
- **VAH / VAL**: High and Low Volume Nodes at +2σ and -2σ from POC. These are fixed anchors for entry triggers.

### Level Mapping:
- **High Volume Node (HVN)**: Top 10% of volume in the anchored range; acts as resistance.
- **Low Volume Node (LVN)**: Bottom 10% of volume in the anchored range; acts as support.
- **Volume Profile Nodes**: Every 5% volume tier from POC to VAH/VAL. Use these for dynamic stop placement and profit targets.

## SECTION 3: Order Flow Footprint Confirmation & Triggers

### Imbalance Requirements:
- **LONG**: Positive delta absorption > 1.2x the average daily imbalance (ADIB). Must be clustered within ±5% of VAH or HVN.
- **SHORT**: Negative delta absorption > 1.2x ADIB. Must be clustered within ±5% of VAL or LVN.

### CVD Divergence:
- LONG: CVD must exceed +0.8% of total volume in the anchored range.
- SHORT: CVD must exceed -0.8% of total volume in the anchored range.

## SECTION 4: Strict Risk Management & Multi-Target Profit Taking

### Stop Placement:
- **Initial Stop**: Place below the last FRVP VAL for LONG, or above the last FRVP VAH for SHORT — no more than 1.5% of total volume from POC.
- **Time-Stop Rule**: If trade stalls >45 minutes without a move toward target, close position and re-enter only after new VWAP + 2σ/ -2σ breach.

### Profit Targets:
- **Target 1 (T1)**: First FRVP node at ±10% from POC. Take partial profit (30%).
- **Target 2 (T2)**: Second FRVP node at ±20% from POC. Take remaining 70%. Trail stop to VWAP.

## SECTION 5: Active Scalper's Daily Routine Checklist

1. Review FRVP anchors for last 3 RTH sessions.
2. Scan 5-minute charts for VWAP + 2σ / -2σ breaches.
3. Confirm order flow imbalances and CVD divergence.
4. Place trades only on confirmed setups — no discretionary entries.
5. Close all positions by 16:00 (4 PM) to avoid overnight risk.
