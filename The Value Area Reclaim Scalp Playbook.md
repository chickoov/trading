# The Value Area Reclaim Scalp

## SECTION 1: Strategic Blueprint & Core Rules (Separated for LONG and SHORT setups)

### LONG Setup:
1. **VWAP Deviation**: Price closes above VWAP +2σ.
2. **POC Breach**: Price must be above POC at the moment of the +2σ breach.
3. **Absorption Confirmation**: Positive delta absorption >1.2× ADIB, with aggressive selling being absorbed (negative delta cluster).
4. **CVD Divergence**: Price makes a lower/equal low while CVD makes a higher low — OR price rises while CVD falls.

### SHORT Setup:
1. **VWAP Deviation**: Price closes below VWAP -2σ.
2. **POC Breach**: Price must be below POC at the moment of the -2σ breach.
3. **Absorption Confirmation**: Negative delta absorption >1.2× ADIB, with aggressive buying being absorbed (positive delta cluster).
4. **CVD Divergence**: Price makes a higher/equal high while CVD makes a lower high — OR price falls while CVD rises.

## SECTION 2: Fixed Range VP Tool Placement & Level Mapping

### Anchoring:
- Anchor FRVP to RTH window or recent impulse wave (15 min–30 min).

### Key Levels:
- **POC**: Price level with highest traded volume.
- **VAH / VAL**: Upper/Lower boundaries of the Value Area — defined as price range containing 70% of total volume.
- **T1 / T2**: Fixed distance from POC: 
   - T1 = ±10% of anchored range’s price distance from POC
   - T2 = ±20% of anchored range’s price distance from POC

## SECTION 3: Order Flow Footprint Confirmation & Triggers (Include exact imbalance percentages/ratios to look for)

### Bullish Absorption:
- **Negative Delta Cluster**: Aggressive selling at/through the level.
- **Price Reclaim**: Price fails to continue downward and subsequently reclaims the level.
- **ADIB Threshold**: Positive delta absorption >1.2× ADIB (aggression being absorbed).

### Bearish Absorption:
- **Positive Delta Cluster**: Aggressive buying at/through the level.
- **Price Rejection**: Price fails to continue upward and subsequently rejects the level.
- **ADIB Threshold**: Negative delta absorption >1.2× ADIB (aggression being absorbed).

### CVD Divergence:
- **LONG**: Price makes a lower/equal low while CVD makes a higher low — OR price rises while CVD falls.
- **SHORT**: Price makes a higher/equal high while CVD makes a lower high — OR price falls while CVD rises.

---

## SECTION 4: Strict Risk Management & Multi-Target Profit Taking (Detail exact stop placement and a time-stop rule for when a trade stalls for 45 minutes)

### Stop Placement:
- **LONG**: Stop = VAL/failed-auction low − 0.1 ATR, provided Entry − Stop ≤ 1.5× ATR.
- **SHORT**: Stop = HVN/failed-auction high + 0.1 ATR, provided Entry − Stop ≤ 1.5× ATR.

### Time-Stop Rule:
- If trade stalls >45 minutes without reaching at least ±0.5R toward target → exit.
- Re-enter only after new VWAP ±2σ breach.

## SECTION 5: Active Scalper's Daily Routine Checklist

1. **Pre-Market**: Review FRVP anchors for RTH window and impulse wave.
2. **Session Start**: Monitor VWAP deviation and POC breaches.
3. **Trade Execution**: Confirm absorption + CVD divergence before entry.
4. **Risk Management**: Place stop within 1.5× ATR buffer; track time-stop rule.
5. **Post-Trade**: Log trade outcome, adjust targets if needed, re-enter only after new VWAP ±2σ breach.
